MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.0.4 - THE ABYSS UPDATE
===========================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.0.4, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.0.4.
Older worlds load fine.


ABYSSAL ARMOR (new)
-------------------
  Recipe (Crafting Table):   B A B      B = Block of Ruby (7)
                             B H B      A = Samurai Armor
                             B B B      H = Abyssal Heart
  - 10 defense
  - +5 damage to EVERYTHING you do (swords, katanas, hammers, bow, musket)
  - +3 extra hearts
  - Crimson-and-void look when you wear it (it uses the Samurai body shape).


ABYSSAL WAR HAMMER (new)
------------------------
  Recipe (Crafting Table):   B B B      B = Block of Ruby (5)
                             B H B      H = Abyssal Heart
                             . S .      S = Sledgehammer (where the stick usually goes)
  - Works like the Sledgehammer, but 14 base damage per smash (the pound does 17).
  - RAGE: every 10th swing it glows purple and for 2 seconds you can swing every
    0.1 seconds.
  - Hammers aren't slowed by the King's melee armour, and it takes Sharpness
    at the Enchanting Table.


THE ABYSSAL HEART
-----------------
  No longer just a trophy: it's what makes Abyssal Armor and the Abyssal War Hammer.
  The King drops one every time you beat him.


BALANCE
-------
  - Katanas (Katana and Sensei's Katana): one attack every 0.5 seconds
    (was 0.22 / 0.19). The dash is unchanged.
  - Tonio's Musket: 45 damage per shot (was 22), and reloads in 1.7 s (was 2.1).


KING OF THE ABYSS: DESPERATION PHASE (new)
------------------------------------------
  Inspired by Calamity's Supreme Calamitas bullet hells. When he drops to 12%
  health he can't be hurt (no single hit can skip it), sacrifices his imps, and
  fights for his life for TWO MINUTES. The boss bar counts down ("survive 1:43").

  How it's built:
    - FAST orbs (11 blocks a second at the start, 16+ at the end; you sprint at
      8.6) in STRUCTURED patterns: walls, rain, fans and rings. No random spam.
    - EVERY wave leaves a way through. In multiplayer EVERY player gets their own
      gap, 2.4-4.2 blocks to one side of them: never where you're standing (you
      have to move), always close enough to reach in time.
    - Starts slow, ends frantic. Near the end the patterns come one at a time,
      faster and faster, instead of piling on top of each other.
    - Walls are warned with white lines first, so you can read the gap.

     0:00-0:18  HELLFIRE VOLLEYS   Fans of 5 fast orbs at each player, plus
                                   Gigablasts: big fireballs that burst into a
                                   ring of orbs.
     0:18-0:36  BRIMSTONE RAIN     Columns of orbs pour from the ceiling with a
                                   gap near you: get under it.
     0:36-0:54  THE WALLS          Walls of orbs sweep in from the sides. The white
                                   lines show the gap: one low line = JUMP it,
                                   no low lines = STAY DOWN.
     0:54-1:12  EVENT HORIZON      Black hole: he sinks to the floor and pulls
                                   harder than you can walk (SPRINT against it),
                                   his purple void hurts a LOT, and fast rings of
                                   orbs come with a gap near each player.
     1:12-1:30  CROSSFIRE          Walls, rain and volleys take turns, plus beams
                                   aimed at you (warned first).
     1:30-1:54  LAST BREATH        Every pattern in turn, as fast as it gets.
     1:54-2:00  ...silence...      He runs out of strength.

  Survive it and he's EXHAUSTED: he sinks to the floor, his melee armour is gone,
  and every hit does 1.5x damage until he dies.

  Testing: a player who reads the rain and the walls took 0 damage from them;
  standing still gets you hit over and over.

  New sounds (Sounds/boss/): beam, purple orb fire, fire orb (Gigablast) fire,
  orb impact (Gigablast bursting).

  DAMAGE: armour takes a flat amount off every hit (Abyssal Armor: 10).
    - Every hit the King lands does 60% more (his normal attacks too).
    - Desperation: orb 16, Gigablast 22, beam 22, the void 24.
      In Abyssal Armor: 6, 12, 12, 14 per hit, out of 26 health.

  To tune it (index.html):
    BOSSES > abyssking > desperation: { at: 0.12, time: 120, exhaustedMult: 1.5 }
    BOSSES > abyssking > dmgMult: 1.6
    DESP_DMG = { orb: 10, beam: 14, voidTouch: 15, blast: 14 }  (before the x1.6)
    DESP_ACTS (when each act starts), and in kingDesperation each pattern's
    pace(slow, fast) numbers (its speed and how often it fires, start -> end).


BEDS SET YOUR SPAWN POINT
-------------------------
  - Right-click a bed at ANY time of day: "Respawn point set". (Sleeping at night
    still skips to morning and heals you.)
  - You respawn next to your bed. If the bed is broken or blocked, you're told so
    and go back to world spawn.
  - A bed inside a building respawns you at that building's front door.
  - In multiplayer, every player has their own bed. It's saved with the world.
