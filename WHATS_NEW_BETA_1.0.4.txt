MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.0.4 - THE ABYSS UPDATE
===========================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.0.4, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.0.4.
Older worlds load fine.


ABYSSAL ARMOR (new)
-------------------
  Recipe (Crafting Table):   B A B      B = Block of Ruby (7)
                             B H B      A = Samurai Armor
                             B B B      H = Abyssal Heart
  - 10 defense
  - +5 damage to EVERYTHING you do (swords, katanas, hammers, bow, musket)
  - +3 extra hearts
  - Crimson-and-void look when you wear it (it uses the Samurai body shape).


ABYSSAL WAR HAMMER (new)
------------------------
  Recipe (Crafting Table):   B B B      B = Block of Ruby (5)
                             B H B      H = Abyssal Heart
                             . S .      S = Sledgehammer (where the stick usually goes)
  - Works like the Sledgehammer, but 14 base damage per smash (the pound does 17).
  - RAGE: every 10th swing it glows purple and for 2 seconds you can swing every
    0.1 seconds.
  - Hammers aren't slowed by the King's melee armour, and it takes Sharpness
    at the Enchanting Table.


THE ABYSSAL HEART
-----------------
  No longer just a trophy: it's what makes Abyssal Armor and the Abyssal War Hammer.
  The King drops one every time you beat him.


BALANCE
-------
  - Katanas (Katana and Sensei's Katana): one attack every 0.5 seconds
    (was 0.22 / 0.19). The dash is unchanged.
  - Tonio's Musket: 45 damage per shot (was 22), and reloads in 1.7 s (was 2.1).


KING OF THE ABYSS: DESPERATION PHASE (new, rebuilt)
---------------------------------------------------
  Inspired by the Desperation phase in Calamity / Infernum (Supreme Calamitas), but a
  lot gentler: he's the first boss.

  When he drops to 1% health he can't be hurt (no single hit can skip it), sacrifices
  his imps and floats to the middle of his arena.

    0:00-0:10  GET READY      Nothing is thrown at you. Reposition. His old song fades
                              out. (The boss bar counts down.)
    0:10       NEW SONG       The Desperation song starts and he fights for 2:30
                              ("survive 2:30" on the boss bar).

  How it works: every attack has a warning you can read and a way through that is
  wider than you are. No random spam, nothing unavoidable. It is FAST and it is HARD:
  projectiles reach 9-13 blocks a second (you sprint at 8.6), the breathers between
  attacks shrink to almost nothing, and in the last two acts two attacks run at once.

    SPIRAL      fast rotating arms of orbs: run with the gap. They reverse once
                (after a short pause).
    FAN         a white line shows where his darts are aimed. He LEADS you: if you
                run in a straight line the darts go where you're going.
    GIGABLAST   big orbs that burst into a ring of orbs, with a hole near you.
    BEAM COMB   vertical beams, shaded with their TRUE width. Then the same row
                shifted over: step between them.
    LANCE       a beam that follows you, then LOCKS: move when it locks.
    WAVE        low orbs sweep the arena along a white line: JUMP them (2, then 3 in
                a row).
    RAIN        orbs fall from the ceiling: the GREEN lane is the safe gap. (The lane
                is always on floor you can reach: never over the lava.)
    SEEKERS     a swarm of cyan orbs that CHASE you. They only turn so fast: run a
                curve, then cut back, or jump at the last moment. From Act II one
                seeker is ALWAYS on your tail, on top of whatever else is happening.

  It builds over five acts:
     0:00 Act I     spiral, fan, rain, wave
     0:22 Act II    + gigablast, beam comb, seekers (and the seeker on your tail)
     0:46 Act III   + lance: everything
     1:14 Act IV    everything, faster, sometimes two attacks at once
     1:44 Finale    everything, as fast as it gets, often two at once
  In multiplayer every player gets their own aim line, gap, jump line and seekers.

  Survive it and he's EXHAUSTED: he sinks to the floor, his melee armour is gone,
  and every hit does 1.5x damage (at 1% health that's one hit).

  New sounds: beam, purple orb fire, fire orb (Gigablast) fire, orb impact (Gigablast
  bursting) in Sounds/boss/. New song: Sounds/music/desperation_BGM.mp3.

  DAMAGE: armour takes a flat amount off every hit (Abyssal Armor: 10).
    - Every hit the King lands does 60% more (his normal attacks too).
    - Desperation: orb 14, Gigablast 19, beam 21.
      In Abyssal Armor: 4, 9, 11 per hit, out of 26 health.

  To tune it (index.html):
    BOSSES > abyssking > desperation: { at: 0.01, prep: 10, time: 150, exhaustedMult: 1.5 }
    BOSSES > abyssking > dmgMult: 1.6
    DESP_DMG = { orb: 9, beam: 13, blast: 12 }  (before the x1.6)
    DESP_ACTS (which attacks each act picks from), and in kingDesperation each
    attack's pace(slow, fast) numbers (its speed and how often it fires, start -> end).


BEDS SET YOUR SPAWN POINT
-------------------------
  - Right-click a bed at ANY time of day: "Respawn point set". (Sleeping at night
    still skips to morning and heals you.)
  - You respawn next to your bed. If the bed is broken or blocked, you're told so
    and go back to world spawn.
  - A bed inside a building respawns you at that building's front door.
  - In multiplayer, every player has their own bed. It's saved with the world.
